The opening buildup storyboard

Five scenes, 20.8 seconds. A quiet evidence-led reveal that establishes Julie as the authority before the masterclass begins. Shot, type, voice, b-roll on the exact word, sound and transition specified per scene.

Scene 1. The claim

00:00.0 TO 00:03.9

THE FEELING
A stranger feels a brief stop of disbelief, then leans in because the claim is calm rather than promotional.
SHOT
Julie, direct to camera, framed in a tight chest-up portrait with her eyes just above center. Use an 85 mm lens at T1.8, camera nearly locked with a 3 percent controlled push-in over the shot. Keep her irises sharp and the studio background fully softened. Place a soft, large key 45 degrees camera left, a narrow cool rim from rear camera right, and one dim amber practical far behind her. Palette is charcoal, midnight navy, skin warmth, and a restrained amber pinprick. No logo, no lower third, no introductory slate.
ON-SCREEN TYPE
Card 1, 33.5% slower than average, centered at lower middle, large condensed white grotesque, 88 percent opacity black gradient behind only the baseline area. Fade in over 6 frames on the word slower, hold through the end of the scene.
VO / SYNC LINE
I age thirty-three point five percent slower than average.
B-ROLL AT WORD
At the first syllable of slower, cut for 10 frames to a macro of Julie’s eye catching the studio key light, then return to her direct gaze before the final word average completes. The eye reflection makes the claim feel observed, not announced.
SOUND
Begin with one low, close cello harmonic and a nearly inaudible room tone. Add a single soft sub hit exactly at 33.5. Dialogue is fully dominant, dry, intimate, and close-miked. No percussion yet.
TRANSITION OUT
Match cut from the circular catchlight in Julie’s eye to the circular assay marker on a printed DunedinPACE report. The shared circle motivates the cut and turns the claim into evidence.

Scene 2. The number

00:03.9 TO 00:09.2

THE FEELING
The stranger moves from curiosity to trust because the claim receives a precise, legible benchmark.
SHOT
Begin on a top-down macro insert of an authentic, cleared-to-use DunedinPACE result document, with the relevant result area in focus. Use a 50 mm macro lens at T4, slider move left to right for 1.2 seconds. Cut to Julie seated at a real desk in three-quarter profile, seen over her shoulder as she studies the page. Use a 50 mm lens at T2.8 and a slow 8 degree orbit toward her face. Light the document with a soft neutral key from overhead and camera left, give Julie a cool rim, and let a shaded window practical create a soft vertical highlight. Palette is paper cream, graphite, desaturated blue, and muted green.
ON-SCREEN TYPE
Card 1, DunedinPACE 0.665, upper left, white sans serif with 0.665 in pale mineral green. Card 2, Average is 1.00, directly below Card 1, 55 percent of Card 1 size, warm gray. Both cards arrive as a single, quiet data readout, with no animated chart.
VO / SYNC LINE
My DunedinPACE is zero point six six five. Average is one point zero zero.
B-ROLL AT WORD
At the first syllable of DunedinPACE, leave Julie and land on the printed report. At the first six in zero point six six five, push into the 0.665 result field. At Average, cut to a clean side-by-side graphic in the same paper texture, 0.665 above 1.00, with the lower figure aligned to the real report marker. At the first zero in one point zero zero, cut back to Julie’s hand resting on the page.

Introduce a second cello note one fifth above the first and a faint pencil-on-paper texture on the first syllable of DunedinPACE. Place a precise, short glass tick at 0.665 and a lower tick at 1.00. Dialogue remains dominant. Do not use medical monitor sounds.
TRANSITION OUT
Dissolve from the diagonal edge of the report into the diagonal edge of a grocery receipt on a kitchen counter. The matching paper texture motivates a proof-to-practicality transition.

Notes for the edit

Every animation ships in four layers so the film can be recut without a re-render. Master is the finished frame. Visuals only removes all type so new copy can be set in the edit. Text on black is a luma matte source. Text alpha is a transparent WebM for direct compositing over footage. All 6.0 seconds, 30 fps, 181 frames, motion blur baked at a 1/62 shutter. Type is Instrument Serif for headlines and Inter Tight 900 for numbers. No gold, no drop shadow, no third colour.

Claim discipline. The No. 2 rank is written in the past tense on every card because it is a held 2023 leaderboard rank. 0.665 against a 1.00 average is 33.5 percent slower. The two-million-dollar comparison is never attached to a name.
