# Julie Gibson Clark · Opening animations · delivery

Native 4K. Every animation ships in four formats and four layers.

**Layers:** Master is everything baked. Visuals only is the motion with no text. Text on black is the words alone. Text alpha is the words with a transparent background, drops straight over footage.
